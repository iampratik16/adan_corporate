# Copy draft, for partner review

Every word written for this site, in one place. **None of it is approved.** It is drawn from the
current site's own content and rewritten to the house rules below; where the current site supports
no claim, none is made.

House rules applied throughout: British English, sentence case, plain and active. No hype words
("world-class", "cutting-edge", "synergy", "bespoke", "seamless", "passionate"). No exclamation
marks. No em-dashes. Nothing invented.

Where copy lives in a typed file, this document points at it rather than duplicating it, so there is
one source of truth and no drift.

---

## Homepage

**H1.** Cross-border corporate finance for the mid-market.

**Supporting line.** Adan Corporate is an international advisory firm of former C-suite executives.
We help growing companies and funds raise capital, buy, sell and transform, from seed funding to
listing.

_Note: the brief drafted "from seed funding to IPO". Changed to "to listing" because the sentence
already carries two abbreviations and IPO appears again two sections below._

**Statement heading.** An advisory firm of people who have run companies, not only advised them.

**Statement body.** Our partners are mostly former chief executives, finance directors and board
members of listed companies. They work with founders, boards and investors in the mid-market, and
most of what they do crosses a border: a company in one market, the capital in another, and a
structure that has to satisfy both.

**Who we work with.**

- _Companies and founders._ You are raising capital, planning a sale or an acquisition, or preparing
  for a listing, and you want partners who have sat on your side of the table.
- _Funds and family offices._ You are placing a fund, sourcing mid-market opportunities across
  borders, or need diligence on a deal in a market you do not cover.
- _Senior professionals._ You have run a company and want to keep working on deals and boards, with
  a partnership rather than an employer.

**AI band heading.** Practical AI, for deals and for the businesses behind them.

**AI band, advising clients.** Where AI changes what a mid-market business is worth, how it is run,
or what a buyer needs to diligence, we advise on it: readiness and strategy, AI and data diligence
in a transaction, governance and risk, and the analytics and automation that follow.

**AI band, in our own work.** We use the same tools ourselves, to source and screen opportunities
across markets, to get through research and diligence faster, and to build and stress financial
models. Partners still take the view. The work is checked before it leaves the firm.

_Note: deliberately narrow. No product, no tool name, no track record, no metric. The current site
supports none of those. This is the section most in need of partner sign-off._

**Transactions.** Client names are withheld. Values are as completed.

**Network heading.** Capital rarely sits in the same place as the company that needs it.

**Network body.** Our value is the route between the two: senior people who already know the
investors, lenders and agencies in the market where the money is.

**The four principles**, rewritten from the old site's "agnostic" cards:

| Old                 | New                                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sector Agnostic     | **Sectors.** From mining and manufacturing to augmented reality and clean energy. We are not tied to a sector. |
| Location Agnostic   | **Geographies.** Offices in 14 cities and partners in several more. Most mandates cross at least one border.   |
| Deal-Value Agnostic | **Deal sizes.** US$1m to US$500m, with the weight of the work in the mid-market.                               |
| Deal-Type Agnostic  | **Deal types.** Equity, debt, a sale, a purchase, a merger, a restructuring or a placement.                    |

_Note: "agnostic" appeared four times on the old homepage and says nothing to a reader. The range is
what carries the point. The deal-size figure is flagged: the published transaction list runs from
$2.5m to $1bn, matching neither end of the stated range._

**People heading.** The people you would actually be working with.

**Insights heading.** Writing and conversation.

_Note: not "Latest insights". Every article is from 2018 or 2019 and calling them latest would be
dishonest._

**Podcast body.** Conversations with founders, investors and advisers about deals that completed,
and what it took to get them there.

**Closing band.** Speak to a partner. / Tell us what you are trying to do and we will put you in
front of the person who has done it before. No call centre, no junior triage.

## Pillars

Full copy in `content/pillars.ts`: five statements, their capability groups with descriptions, and
the "how we work" sequences.

The old site had **no pillar-level positioning copy at all**; its four index pages are link grids of
roughly 150 sub-service stubs. So every statement, every capability description and every sequence
is new writing, built from the sub-service prose. The service names, groupings and specific items
are taken from the source.

Descriptors, fixed by the brief:

| Pillar                 | Descriptor                                                |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Corporate Finance      | Raising equity and debt across borders                    |
| Mergers & Acquisitions | Buying, selling and combining businesses                  |
| Strategy & Leadership  | Senior counsel for boards and management teams            |
| Risk & Governance      | Control, compliance and resilience                        |
| AI & Digital           | Practical AI for deals and for the businesses behind them |

Coaching sits inside Strategy & Leadership and gets no pillar and no sub-pages. Startups are an
audience of Corporate Finance, not a pillar. COVID-19 services, blockchain, IoT and RPA are dropped.

## Transactions

All 36 headlines in `content/transactions.ts`. Each is one sentence in a consistent structure: what
Adan did, for what kind of business, where. Deals stay anonymised.

Superlatives the source asserts but cannot evidence are softened: "Asia's largest film production
and distribution firm" becomes "one of Asia's largest". Nothing is strengthened, nothing is dropped.
Source typos are fixed ("consultany", curly-quote artefacts).

Corridors are derived from the source's own Deal Region column and nothing else.

## People

All 32 biographies in `content/people.ts`, carried from the profile pages and cleaned:

- typos fixed ("in in C-suite", "mid-sizes", "Sout East Asia", "GEMS eduction", missing spaces)
- US spellings converted ("optimization", "organization", "specializes", "Modernization")
- trimmed to 40 to 90 words, keeping the seniority signal and dropping the list-of-everything tail

**No fact, figure, employer, qualification or date was changed.**

Where a person's expertise taxonomy was byte-identical to another partner's and contradicted their
own biography, it is **withheld rather than republished**. Those profiles show no expertise tags
until the client supplies a real list.
