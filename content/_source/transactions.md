## 4. FLAGS

**FLAG-C1 — Clients p., deal 5 breaks the value ordering.** Every other card descends by Deal Size ($1 billion → $200 million → … → $5 million), but "Strategic sale for an Industrial Gases company. — $ 2.5 million" sits at position 5, between the $1 billion and the $200 million cards. Either the figure is wrong (should it be $2.5 billion or $250 million?) or the card is mis-placed. Query with the client before republishing.

**FLAG-C2 — Clients p., deal 33 has an empty Sector(s) field.** "MBO of Green building materials self-certification SaaS firm" renders a "Sector(s):" label with no tags under it. Which sector(s)?

**FLAG-C3 — Clients p., the same sentence is published twice with a grammatical error in one copy.** The raw HTML contains both "Our clients rely on us **to** help them solve some of their toughest challenges..." and "Our clients rely on us help them solve some of their toughest challenges..." (two responsive variants of the same pull quote; one is missing "to").

**FLAG-C4 — Clients p., typo.** Deal 6: "multinational technical **consultany** firm" (should read "consultancy").

**FLAG-C5 — Clients p., grammar.** Body copy: "one **the** world's leading advertising groups" (deal 34) and "we have enabled our clients realize new business opportunities" (missing "to").

**FLAG-C6 — Clients p., dated claims.** Deals 11, 13 and 23 carry hard years — "in 2015", "in 2016", "in 1992" — and superlatives ("the world's largest VFX and 3D firm in 2015"). Confirm these still stand before reuse.

**FLAG-C7 — Duplicate superlative.** Deal 8 ("Asia's largest private mining company with the largest investment in renewable energy", $150m) and deal 12 ("Asia's largest private company with the largest investment in renewable energy", $77m) describe what looks like the same client with two different superlatives. Same client, two mandates? Or a copy/paste error?

**FLAG-L1 — Currency of the "live" deals is unverifiable from the page.** The mandates are hardcoded in static HTML with no date field and no last-updated stamp, and the footer reads "© Adan Corporate 2013 - 2021." **The client must confirm which of these 31 mandates are still live before any of them appear on the new site.**

**FLAG-L2 — Mandate ID numbers are non-contiguous and run to 96.** Live IDs present: 1, 7, 17, 18, 21, 33, 38, 46, 50, 51, 57, 60, 62, 64, 67, 68, 71, 73, 76, 77, 83, 86, 87, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 93, 95, 96. Roughly 65 numbers in the 1–96 range are missing — presumably closed or withdrawn mandates deleted from the table. Confirm the numbering scheme is intended to be public.

**FLAG-L3 — Three mandates are unreachable by the Deal Size filter.** Deals 90, 73 and 18 carry `data-dealamount="< $3 mn"`, but "< $3 mn" is not an option in the Deal Size dropdown (which starts at "$3 mn to $10mn"). They can only ever be found with the filter set to "Any". Broken on the old site; must be fixed on the new one.

**FLAG-L4 — Two Deal Types are unreachable by the Deal Type filter.** Deal 67 is "Divestment" and deal 90 is "Business Valuation"; neither appears in the Deal Type dropdown.

**FLAG-L5 — Deal Type contradicts Description on four rows.**
- 87, 86, 83 are typed "Sale" but described as "Equity financing **for the acquisition of** …" — buy-side or sell-side?
- 21 is typed "Equity Raise" (band $10–50mn, amount 30 mn) but described as "Business valuation of an ecommerce company" — a valuation engagement, not a raise.
- 51 is typed "Equity Raise" but described as "Equity **/ Debt** financing".

**FLAG-L6 — Character corruption in four descriptions.** The apostrophes have been replaced with literal double-quote characters in the source: `India"s`, `world"s` (twice), `Geneva"s`. Transcribed as found above; correct to `'` on the new site.

**FLAG-L7 — Deal 88 has a duplicated HTML attribute.** `data-dealtype="Equity Raise"` appears twice on the same `<tr>`. Harmless, but evidence the table was hand-maintained.

**FLAG-L8 — Deals 86 and 83 look like near-duplicates.** "Equity financing for the acquisition of three luxury Swiss Hotels for sale, either individually or as a group." (36 mn) and "…three luxury Swiss **Alps** Hotels for sale, either individually or as a group." (20 mn). Two genuinely separate portfolios, or one mandate entered twice at two prices?

**FLAG-L9 — Amounts sit exactly on band boundaries.** 10 mn → "$3 mn to $10mn" (deals 96, 71), 100 mn → "$50 mn to $100mn" (17, 33), 200 mn → "$100 mn to $200mn" (91), 50 mn → "$10 mn to $50mn" (1, 64), 3 mn → "< $3 mn" (18). The bands are inclusive at the top; deal 18's 3 mn contradicts its own "< $3 mn" band. Define the boundaries unambiguously on the new site.

**FLAG-L10 — Most filter options match no data.** Of 30 Industry options only 13 are used; of 12 Region options only 5 are used; of 18 Deal Type options only 7 are used (plus 2 unlisted ones). Filtering to an unused option silently returns nothing. Decide whether the new site drives filter options from the data.

**FLAG-X1 — The two pages use incompatible taxonomies** for sector and region (detailed at the end of §3). A single controlled vocabulary is needed.

**FLAG-X2 — Hero CTA renders with empty parentheses** on both pages: "Read More About How Adan Corporate (  ) Can Assist". Something was meant to sit inside — presumably an animated logo mark. Ask the client what belonged there.

**FLAG-X3 — All figures are USD with no date.** Deal values span 1992 to at least 2016 with no indication of whether figures are nominal or inflation-adjusted, and no deal dates on the Live Deals table at all.
