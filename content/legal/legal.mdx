## Guidance on Corporate Finance Activities by ICAEW

Adan operates under the guidance on Corporate Finance Activities by ICAEW - Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales: [Link](https://www.icaew.com/-/media/corporate/archive/files/technical/financial-services/investment-business-dpb-fsa/examples-of-corporate-finance.ashx?la=en)

The purpose of this schedule is to describe some common corporate finance scenarios and identify whether these are regulated activities or not and if so whether a firm needs FSA authorisation, a DPB licence, or are unregulated and so no authorisation or licence is needed. Section A of the table refers to general situations, whilst Section B provides more detailed examples.
